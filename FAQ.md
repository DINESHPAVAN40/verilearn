# Frequently Asked Questions

## Getting Started

### Q: Is VeriLearn free?
A: Yes! VeriLearn is completely free to use.

### Q: Do I need to install anything?
A: No, VeriLearn is web-based. Just visit our website and start learning!

### Q: What browser should I use?
A: We recommend modern browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

## Learning

### Q: What topics are covered?
A: We cover basics, combinational logic, sequential logic, and advanced topics like state machines.

### Q: Can I download the guides?
A: Yes, you can save Verilog files directly from the editor.

### Q: Are there video tutorials?
A: Currently guides are text-based. Video tutorials are planned for v2.2.

## Projects & Saving

### Q: Can I save my work?
A: Yes! Create an account, and all your projects are saved automatically.

### Q: Can I delete projects?
A: Yes, you can delete projects from the workspace sidebar.

### Q: Can I share my code?
A: Code sharing is planned for v2.2.

## Technical

### Q: What is Verilog?
A: Verilog is a Hardware Description Language (HDL) used to design digital circuits.

### Q: Can I simulate my code?
A: Currently, we validate syntax. Full simulation is coming in v2.3.

### Q: Is my code validated?
A: Yes, we check for basic syntax errors when you click "Validate".

## Account

### Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page.

### Q: Can I change my email?
A: Email change is planned for v2.1.

### Q: Is my data private?
A: Yes, your data is private and protected by Firebase security rules.

## Troubleshooting

### Q: I can't sign up
A: Check your internet connection and ensure your email is valid.

### Q: My projects disappeared
A: Try refreshing the page. If problem persists, contact support.

### Q: The editor isn't loading
A: Clear your browser cache and try again.
